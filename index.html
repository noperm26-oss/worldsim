WORLDSIM

A world of nothing but clear water, and you.

You are a god. Your currency is Faith. You raise land, paint biomes, breathe the first two people into being — and then you watch. They farm, build, found towns, discover writing and bronze and gunpowder, take up religions, trade, betray each other and go to war. You never place a building.
